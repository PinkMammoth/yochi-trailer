# YOCHI: flagship trailer (director's cut of THE CAST)

**One line:** everyone in crypto is a type with an opinion; in Yochi those opinions are picks, the picks are
played for real, the winners get stamped, and the best players wear the best faces. The game becomes the brand.

THE CAST asked "which one am I?". The flagship asks "I want to play this", and keeps the characters as the hook.
Same track, same 54.6 s, same first frame. Built in `film-flagship/` from a copy of `film-cast/` (which is
untouched, as are its deliverables on `claude/gracious-bell-72as9d`).

repeating the roster joke.

### The final second, from TEN SECONDS (17.74 to 19.16)

The duel keeps its speed. From 0:02 the board **strips away**: the band, the buttons, the history candles and the
caption fall back into the dark; the camera pushes in on the live candle and the strike tag (a macro push, 1 to
2.1×). The **margin** is drawn: a hairline bracket between the candle's close and the dashed strike line, labelled
in mono `−0.006`. The candle is a hair *under* the line: you're losing. The huge arcade timer stays in frame,
**0:01**, with its vermilion drain bar a sliver. Then the existing hard dropout: 0.87 s of nothing. On the drop
kick the candle snaps closed UP, `+0.075`, the board snaps back to full, and CALLED IT lands on the next kick as
before. One change, carefully judged: the freeze now *means* something, because you can see how close it is.

### The reward gallery (38.79 to 40.48, one bar)

After four single heroes (Cold Blooded, Duel Master, Grudge Settled, Whale, two beats each, unlock rules as

ROYALES). Then the 18-slot grid lights the rest on sixteenths with the caption. The message ("you earn better

## Music

Same track, same edit as THE CAST (`Victory Stamp.mp3` from source bar 2, the 0:01 dropout, the tail fade). Every
scene time is still on the measured grid (142.3 BPM, bar 1.6866 s); only the event map in `src/core/music.js`
changes. The takes land on four eighths of film bar 7 (the build), the strike line on its third beat, the drop on
bar 8 (unchanged). The tear starts on the half-bar after the press; the gather runs through bar 27; the seal lands
on bar 28's downbeat, where the bass drops out, so the stamp is the hit.

**Sound design** (synthesised in code, mixed low, `tools/sfx.mjs`, switchable with `--sfx 0`): a dry paper-brush
swish under each tear stroke (band-passed noise, fast attack); a felt thump plus a short paper slap for each
stamp contact (CALLED IT ×2, the final seal). If these sound synthetic against the track, the right replacement is
a recorded brush-on-washi pass and a real hanko/wood-block stamp; see the README.

## Decisions and open questions

1. **Act II is one bar, not two.** Two bars of type turned into a second roster; one bar is a beat of the joke
   becoming the mechanic.
2. **The takes carry faces**, so they read as the cast's voices, not a new world.
3. **The ten-seconds change is the freeze, not the timer.** The duel still moves at THE CAST's speed; only the last
   0.55 s before the freeze and the freeze itself change.
4. **The seal lands on the same downbeat as THE CAST's end card.** The ink signature takes its time from the
   ladder grid (one bar) and the landing page (one bar earlier), so the end card keeps its 7.4 s.
5. **The gather is a mask, the mark is the asset.** The Y is the official SVG; the ink only reveals it.
6. **No faded cast behind the end card** (THE CAST had it): the signature needs a clean sheet.
