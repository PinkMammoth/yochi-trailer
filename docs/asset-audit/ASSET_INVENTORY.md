The full per-file list (662 assets: path, branch, dimensions, transparency, duration, first/last commit, author,

## How dates were judged

- **Filesystem dates are useless here.** Every file in the checkout has the clone time (14 Nov 22:19). All dating
  comes from Git.
- **Authorship is a strong signal.** Commits by the owner (`Matthew Bentman` at +0900, `PinkMammoth`) add source
  assets; commits by `Claude` build films from them. An owner-added asset outranks a Claude-made one.
- **Brand timeline from history:**
  - 27 Sep to 17 Oct: the September cyan trailer (`film/`, `asset-pack/characters|avatars|props|raw-clips`, `stills/`), merged as PRs 1–8.
  - 17 Oct: a 予知 sumi-e experiment (`claude/keen-rubin-vpzk4u`), made before the kit existed.
  - **28 Oct: the Sumi kit lands** (`8a2fc71`, `asset-pack/sumi/`, with SHARED-RULES: "that film is the retired brand").
  - 30 Oct: Gunslinger retired, Ronin added (`99cb12b`).
  - 3 Nov: the owner adds the Victory Stamp track and the v2 concept prompts.
  - 3–5 Nov: THE CAST, then the flagship (**approved**), the final edit, the first ad, the hook (**approved**), the caption fix (**approved**), Option A, then **`yochi-first-ad-final`, "the locked film"**.
  - 5 Nov: the owner adds **v2 paintings of Perma-Bull and Cold Blooded**, but only into `film-flagship/assets/cast/source/` on the flagship branches.
  - 5–7 Nov: the YOCHI × ELYSIUM cards (landscape v3 **approved**), owner-supplied official Elysium marks, the owner's 4K capture pack, the gameplay trailers.
  - 14 Nov: the USDC short (v1, under review).
  - 15 Nov: the owner confirms v2 Perma-Bull and Cold Blooded as canon (now in the kit), HYPE as the Elysium stake,
    the kanji review as done, and the casting for the USDC rework. No merge to `master` yet.
- **`master` stops at 3 Nov.** None of the Sumi films are merged. Approval is recorded in the branches' own READMEs
  ("the approved flagship", "the approved cut", "The locked film"), not by merges. That's adequate evidence, but
  it's indirect (see OPEN_DECISIONS).

## Gaps

- **No standalone environment or background art.** The Sumi world is the paper and sumi grounds (procedural washi
  fibre: `sumi.css` and `draw.js`), each painting's own ink-disc plate, and rebuilt UI. There are no stage
  paintings, skylines or rooms.
- **No raster parchment or ink textures.** Paper fibre, seal stamp texture and the bokashi wash are all procedural.
  There are no scanned washi or ink-splash files.
- **No real foley.** Stamp thumps and brush swishes are synthesised stand-ins. The flagship README asks for a
  recorded brush-on-washi pass and a real hanko stamp.
- **The canonical layered cast lives off-`master`,** on `claude/yochi-first-ad-order-a`.
