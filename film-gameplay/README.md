## The idea

**The game explains itself.** No abstract montage and no invented world: the real screens, cut hard on the beat
the way a fighting game cuts its versus screens, with one plain-English line per shot so it works muted. The
product's own home page asks the opening question (*Where does the next HYPE candle close?*); the Duel shows the
answer is a pick, UP or DOWN, against a clock; the Battle Royale goes silent on "One candle decides." and the
verdict band (▲ CLOSED UP) lands on the drop kick; a win stamps down; then three quick beats of the social layer
(Nemesis, rankings, clans) before the co-branded hanko stamps onto the end card on the bass dip.

Both films share the core edit, the track, the type and the end card. They differ where their jobs differ:

| | A: testnet (try it free) | B: mainnet (it's live, real money) |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | the question (home page), then the Duel | **cold open on a win**: the CALLED IT stamp lands on the first bass hit, "Win USDC on the next candle.", then the question |
| The pick | two bars on the board, "Pick UP or DOWN." then "Read it right, take the pot." | one bar (the cold open bought its time) |
| Battle Royale | "Every wrong pick costs a life." | "Everyone stakes into one pot." |
| After the drop | Last one standing: **"Free to play, no real money."** | Last one standing: **"Call it right. Take the pool."** (the tagline) |
| Second win | Free For All: "The right side splits the pool." | the Duel resolves: You took the pot, +19, "Winner takes the pot." |
| Badge (top right, whole film) | ELYSIUM TESTNET · FREE PLAY | LIVE ON ELYSIUM |
| End card | PLAY YOCHI ON ELYSIUM TESTNET · **Try it free.** · testnet.yochigg.xyz · TEST FUNDS ONLY · NO REAL MONEY | YOCHI IS LIVE ON ELYSIUM · **Predict. Compete. Win.** · yochigg.xyz · 18+ · REAL-MONEY GAMEPLAY · PLAY RESPONSIBLY |

Both carry a small **GAMEPLAY DEMO · FICTIONAL PLAYERS** line bottom left for the whole of the gameplay, because
the pack's copy rule is that no clip is presented as a real match, result or player.

## Footage choices

Used: C1 (standoff and board), C3, C5, C6, C13, C14, and the home, leaderboard, clans and duel-recap stills.

Left out, and why:
- **C2a / C2b (MATCH POINT / FINAL ROUND):** the clock in those clips is *picks open in*, not a candle closing, so
  cutting them into the tension beat would misstate what the timer means. The C1 board shows the real pick window.
- **C4 (FFA loss):** honest, but a loss doesn't earn its second in a 24 s ad.
- **C7 (BR final two):** the same rektless × hedged standoff as C1; using both reads as a repeat.
- **C8 to C12 (level-ups, achievements):** striking, but they explain a second system, and their glyphs (凡 民 商 影
  氷) are outside the approved kanji list. Progression is the next feature to add if a longer cut is wanted.
- **Landing still:** its LIVE NOW ticker shows fixture numbers that would read as real player activity.

Nothing is redrawn or altered in any shot. Shots are trimmed, retimed (only the C6 board runs at 0.7x, into the
silence, so the verdict band lands on the drop), framed and cut. The board shots are cropped above y 742 so the
chat fixture (with legacy sticker emotes) never appears.

## Framing and scaling

The pack is named "4k" but the clips are 1920×1080 (game screens) and 1600×900 (moment cards); the stills are
2880×1800 (2x). Output is 1920×1080, the project's standard and the footage's native size, which is also the best
X format for a 16:9 UI. Scaling, per shot: game screens 1.0 to at most 1.24 (the push to the UP / DOWN buttons) and
1.22 to 1.30 (the roll call and verdict); moment cards 1.2 (to fill 1080p) to at most 1.36 (the Nemesis panel);
stills are downscaled (0.6 to 0.86). The pushes are slow and short; the UI stays sharp enough to read.

## Type

Dela Gothic One for the supers (78 px, cap height ~56 px at 1080p, above the 48 px mobile floor), JetBrains Mono for
kickers, the badge and the small print. A sumi (or paper) gradient sits under each super. Vermilion is only on the
kicker tick, the slash wipes, the badge outline and the URL. The film adds no kanji of its own.

## Music and sound

`asset-pack/audio/Victory Stamp.mp3`, the track of every approved Sumi film, on the flagship's measured grid
(`src/core/music.js`). Three whole-bar segments, spliced with 12 ms equal-power crossfades on downbeats: source
bars 3 to 7 (from the bass entry), 11 to 19 (the build's last two bars, the swell cut to silence, the drop and the
groove), and 30 onward (the bass dip, faded over the end card). Verified on the built audio: silence 9.89 to 10.73,
the drop kick at 10.73, no level step at either splice, −15.7 LUFS integrated.

Sound design (`tools/sfx.mjs`, the flagship's synthesised voices, mixed low): a hanko thump at each on-screen stamp
contact and the end-card seal, and a dry-brush swish under the two slash wipes. The clips are silent captures, so
there is no UI audio.

## Build

```sh
cd film-gameplay
node tools/frames.mjs                      # extract the source frames both edits use into cache/ (~70 MB)
node tools/render.mjs --v A                # lossless render, out/A_lossless.mkv (~9 min on 4 cores)
node tools/audio.mjs A out/A.wav           # soundtrack (track edit + sfx)
tools/deliver.sh out/A_lossless.mkv out/A.wav deliverables yochi-elysium-testnet
tools/sheet.sh deliverables/yochi-elysium-testnet.mp4 deliverables/yochi-elysium-testnet-contact.jpg
node tools/still.mjs out/stills A 0.8 10.8 21   # any moment as PNG
```

Same for `B` / `yochi-elysium-mainnet`. Needs ffmpeg and Playwright's Chromium (global install, as for the
cards). Every frame is a pure function of t, as in the other films. Layout and copy for both edits are in
`src/edl.js`; the end card is `src/endcard.js` (the approved v3 landscape card with a copy/layout block added).

## For review before publishing

- **Mainnet URL:** B points to `yochigg.xyz`, the site named in the brand rules. If the Elysium mainnet build
  lives elsewhere, change `EDITS.B.card.url` and re-render the last 4.4 s.
- **"Predict. Compete. Win."** is the brief's suggested line; it breaks the Sumi copy rule against "X. Y."
  slogans. Kept because it was asked for; easy to swap.
- **Real-money compliance:** B's "18+ · real-money gameplay · play responsibly" line is a placeholder for whatever
  wording and jurisdictions Yochi's counsel requires. A says "test funds only · no real money" twice.
- **The footage is demo data**: fixture players, real HYPE candles. The disclosure line covers this; the shots
  are presented as how the game plays, not as real matches.
- **Kanji in the product UI** that appear in shot: 対, 勝, 連勝 (approved), plus 残 (BR header), 敵 (the Nemesis
  seal) and 組 (clans). They're the app's own UI, but they're outside the approved list pending the November review.
- **Vertical (4:5 / 9:16) not made.** The UI is a 16:9 desktop layout; a vertical cut needs the mobile captures the
  pack README mentions (`mobile/*.mp4`), which are not in the repository.
- **Collectible avatars and global chat** are not shown as features: no clip shows avatar collection on its own, and
  the only chat footage is the fixture panel with legacy stickers.
